## Scope

The official FAQ supports principles concerning meaning, part of speech, technical terms, conditions, voice, and `-ing` forms. Summaries of sentence length, paragraphs, noun clusters, and punctuation draw on the reference project's rule summaries. Formal review requires the specified edition's complete rules. Relevant sections are Issue 9, Part 1, Sections 2, 4, 5, 6, and 8.

General reporting structure, Chinese expression, and bilingual comparison form this skill's workflow. Formal STE review uses the specified standard and dictionary.
